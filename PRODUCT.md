# Atlas

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People exploring geographic reporting and evidence, including public users, journalists, and investors researching documented impacts.

## Product Purpose

Explore sourced news on a globe, inspect verified investigations and dated observations, and ask evidence-backed questions. `atlas_prd.md` remains the product source of truth.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React/TypeScript/Vite, Mapbox GL JS, Zustand, TanStack Query, shared contracts, same-origin Fastify API. Keep dates, units, geographic precision, provenance, and historical fixture labels honest. No invented claims, live integrations, or building identification. Browser filters and selection are authoritative. Credentials stay server-side except the restricted public Mapbox token.

## Brand Commitments

The user selected Night Earth · Dark: minimal Apple-inspired controls, opaque charcoal panels, system typography, a centered complete globe, Search/News dock, one on-demand panel. Use dark cartography rather than real night imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

Verified Helene seed records and observations in `db/seeds/helene-asheville-2024`, source-backed imagery in `assets/evidence/helene-asheville-2024`, shared historical examples in `contracts/examples`.

## Product Principles

- Geography leads; content opens on demand.
- One panel preserves a clear exploration context.
- Sources and uncertainty remain accessible.
- Missing data is never replaced with fabricated evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, reduced motion, readable contrast, chart table alternatives, responsive sheets, and safe-area support.
